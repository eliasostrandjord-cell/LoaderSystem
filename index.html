Sonic CD Pocket

The 2011 Sonic CD for iPhone · runs on your own game files
What to pick (from the Files app), from a copy you own:
• Data.rsdk from the Steam version (Steam → Sonic CD → Manage → Browse local files), or
• the Android .apk of Sonic CD: the game data is taken out of it right here on the phone.

Best experience: Share → Add to Home Screen, open it from there and turn the phone sideways. Game controllers work too.
Engine: RSDKv3 decompilation by Rubberduckycooly & st×tic, original RSDK by Christian Whitehead. Not affiliated with SEGA. No game data is included; your file stays on this device. Plus DLC disabled.
